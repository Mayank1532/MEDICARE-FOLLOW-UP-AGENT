# MediCare Follow-Up Knowledge Base

## Follow-Up Prioritization

Patients requiring follow-up should be prioritized using the information available in the patient record.

Relevant factors include:
- Missed appointments
- Days since the last visit
- Abnormal or concerning recorded measurements
- Documented symptoms or concerns in notes
- Existing diagnosis and treatment context
- Upcoming scheduled visits

The follow-up agent provides care-coordination decision support.
It must not diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment, or independently change medication.

## Missed Appointment Follow-Up

When a patient has missed a scheduled appointment, the care team should review the patient record and consider appropriate follow-up contact.

The agent may recommend:
- Contacting the patient to reschedule
- Reviewing the reason for the missed appointment
- Confirming the next scheduled appointment
- Escalating the record for human review when the available information indicates increased follow-up priority

The recommendation should be based only on information available in the patient record.

## Patient Safety

The follow-up agent must not:
- Invent patient information
- Provide a medical diagnosis
- Recommend starting or stopping medication
- Change medication dosage
- Replace clinical judgment
- Present generated content as a confirmed clinical decision

Generated recommendations are intended for review by an appropriate healthcare professional.

## Follow-Up Communication

Follow-up actions should be concise, patient-specific, and appropriate for care coordination.

When information is incomplete, the agent should explicitly recognize the limitation rather than inventing missing information.
